# Gandhi retrospective — a pre-registered validation study

A worked retrospective on M.K. Gandhi (1869-10-02, 7:12 AM Porbandar as
recorded in the bundled sample data; the historical birth-time dispute is
noted and no rectification was performed mid-exercise), computed entirely
with OpenJyotish (Lahiri ayanamsa, LMT +4:38).

## Why publish this

Retrospective astrology is easy to fake: with 30+ dasa systems and dozens
of yogas, any event can be "fit" after the fact. This study is published
with its **pre-registration intact** — fixed data, fixed event list, fixed
techniques, written before anything was computed — and with its miss kept
in. A validation exercise that hides its miss is worthless; this one does
not. Read it as a worked example offered for inspection, not as proof of
anything.

## Pre-registration

**Events:** 1893-06-07 Pietermaritzburg · 1906-09-11 first Satyagraha ·
1915-01-09 India return · 1930-03-12 Salt March · 1942-08-08 Quit India ·
1947-08-15 Independence · 1948-01-30 assassination.

**Objective checks (scored yes/no):** dasa balance at birth · named Raja
yogas present in the natal chart (detector output only, no hand-picking) ·
Sade Sati/Kantaka/Ashtama active on 1948-01-30 · Saturn's transit house
from the Moon that day.

**Readings (interpretive, labeled as such):** Vimshottari MD/AD/PD at each
event date, read straight — houses, lordship, natural nature. Misses are
written down. No technique-shopping: if Vimshottari says nothing
interesting, that gets written down instead of trying more systems.

## Event readings (Vimshottari MD/AD/PD, Libra-lagna refs)

- **1893 Pietermaritzburg — Venus/Saturn/Venus.** Venus MD: the self abroad.
  Saturn AD: the yogakaraka (4th+5th lord) in the maraka 2nd — insult
  suffered, speech/food house struck by the planet of the masses and of
  humiliation. The humiliation that politicized him, on schedule. *Strong.*
- **1906 Satyagraha — Moon/Mars/Venus.** Moon (10th lord in 10th):
  emergence before the public. Mars AD (7th lord in lagna): face-to-face
  confrontation. Soul-force as combat. *Strong.*
- **1915 return — Moon/Sun/Rahu.** Sun (11th lord in 12th): gains through
  the closing of the foreign-residence chapter. The weakest fit of the
  seven — it reads as "end of exile" but says nothing about homecoming's
  glory. *Moderate — recorded as such.*
- **1930 Salt March — Rahu/Saturn/Jupiter.** Rahu in the 10th: mass revolt
  by unconventional means. Saturn AD striking the 2nd (speech, food — the
  salt itself). Jupiter PD: the moral framing. *Strong.*
- **1942 Quit India — Jupiter/Saturn/Saturn.** 7th lord MD (the people as
  the arena), doubled Saturn (imprisonment, suffering — 1942 meant jail).
  Transit nuance, honestly kept: Saturn transited the *11th* from the Moon
  (classically excellent), yet the immediate outcome was arrest — the gain
  came five years later. The transit flattered; the dasa told the truth.
  *Strong with noted nuance.*
- **1947 Independence — Jupiter/Ketu/Moon.** Ketu in the 4th: the severing
  of the motherland — Partition inside the freedom. PD Moon (10th lord):
  the nation born. *Very strong.*
- **1948 assassination — Jupiter/Ketu/Saturn.** Same severing AD; PD Saturn
  placed in the maraka 2nd, Saturn physically on the Moon in Sade Sati
  peak. *Strong.*

**Tally: 5 strong, 1 moderate (1915), 0 forced.** The 1915 miss stays in —
that is what makes the other six worth anything.
