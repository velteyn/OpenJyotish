# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Exalted Mars cancellation

Mars in Capricorn (exaltation) SHALL weaken Kuja Dosha like Mars in
its own signs.

#### Scenario: Exalted Mars
- **WHEN** Mars occupies Capricorn in a dosha house
- **THEN** the dosha is weakened

### Requirement: Whole-sign Jupiter conjunction

A Jupiter–Mars conjunction SHALL cancel Kuja Dosha when both occupy
the same sign, regardless of orb, matching the whole-sign aspect rule.

#### Scenario: Wide conjunction
- **WHEN** Jupiter and Mars share a sign more than 5° apart
- **THEN** the dosha is cancelled
