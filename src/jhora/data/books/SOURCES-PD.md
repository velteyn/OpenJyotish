# Public-domain seed library — provenance record

Every file in this directory ships in the public repo, the wheel and the
frozen apps. Each entry below is pre-1929 publication (US public domain)
with independently checkable rights evidence. NEVER add a text here
without extending this file: title, translator, year, publisher, source
URL and the exact rights statement found.

## 1. brihat-jataka-iyer-1885.txt

- Work: Brihat Jataka of Varahamihira (Sanskrit original c. 6th century)
- Translation: N. Chidambaram Iyer
- Published: 1885, Foster Press, Madras (Thompson & co. reprint 1905 same text)
- Source file: `brihatjatakavar00iyergoog_djvu.txt` via
  https://archive.org/details/brihatjatakavar00iyergoog
  (Harvard digitization of the 1885 edition)
- Rights evidence (all three agree):
  - Wellcome Collection catalogue entry for this edition:
    "Licence: Public Domain Mark"
    (https://wellcomecollection.org/works/afmgm695)
  - archive.org metadata: "Possible copyright status NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT"
  - Google's scan boilerplate inside the file itself: "It has survived
    long enough for the copyright to expire and the book to enter the
    public domain."

## 2. brihat-samhita-iyer-1884.txt

- Work: Brihat Samhita of Varahamihira (Sanskrit original c. 6th century)
- Translation: N. Chidambaram Iyer (same translator as above; title page
  names him Founder of the Tiruvadi Jotistantra Sabha)
- Published: 1884 (archive.org metadata `date: 1884`, item `dli.csl.4675`,
  Central Secretariat Library source record)
- Source file: `4675_djvu.txt` via
  https://archive.org/details/dli.csl.4675
- Rights evidence: 1884 publication is pre-1929 (US public domain) by
  over four decades; same translator, press and era as (1), whose 1885
  edition carries an explicit Public Domain Mark (Wellcome) and a
  NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT record (archive.org). Contemporary documentation of
  the Iyer 1880s series: Rare Books Society of India,
  https://www.rarebooksocietyofindia.org/postDetail.php?id=196174216674_10153566519651675

## 3. surya-siddhanta-burgess-1860.txt

- Work: Surya Siddhanta (Sanskrit siddhanta literature, ancient)
- Translation: Rev. Ebenezer Burgess, "Translation of the Surya-Siddhanta,
  a text-book of Hindu astronomy", Journal of the American Oriental
  Society vol. 6 (1858–1860)
- Published: 1860, American Oriental Society, New Haven
- Source file: `2015.96668.Translation-Of-The-Surya-siddhanta_djvu.txt` via
  https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.96668
- Rights evidence: Digital Library of India record states
  `dc.rights: In Public Domain`; identical 1861 Baptist Mission Press
  edition (Bapu Deva Sastri) hosted proofread on en.wikisource.org,
  which only hosts public-domain texts.

## What is deliberately NOT here

Modern translations and commentaries (Santhanam, Raman, Rao, Rath and
all post-1928 editions): still under copyright regardless of the age of
the Sanskrit original. "Free download" pages grant personal reading, not
redistribution. Those stay in the user's private books folder only.
